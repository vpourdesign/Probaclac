FAQ

Your questions,
our answers

Everything you need to know before starting your treatment.

Can I use Probaclac during my menstrual cycle?
It is best to wait until the end of your cycle before starting treatment. Menstrual flow prevents the probiotics from exerting their beneficial effect — the good bacteria are flushed out and cannot rebalance vaginal pH.
Can it be used during antibiotic therapy?
If you are taking oral antibiotics, you can use Probaclac — just be sure to wait at least 2 to 3 hours before or after. If the antibiotics are administered vaginally, wait until the treatment is complete.
Can pregnant women use it?
The safety of probiotics has been established. However, it is recommended to consult a healthcare professional before use if you are pregnant or breastfeeding.
Can I have sexual intercourse during probiotic therapy?
Yes, absolutely. However, it is recommended to wait until after intercourse to insert the daily capsule to ensure optimal effectiveness.
Does Probaclac Yeast Infection need to be refrigerated?
No. Probaclac Yeast Infection does not require any refrigeration. Simply store it in a cool, dry place. Convenient for travel and everyday use.
What is the recommended dosage?
For adult women (18 years and older): use one capsule per day for 6 consecutive days. Take at least 2 to 3 hours before or after antibiotics. Repeat as needed. See complete instructions on the box.
